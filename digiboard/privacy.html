Privacy Policy for Digi Nomad's SoundBoard +Memes

Last Updated: September 3, 2026

The short version

  • The app has no accounts and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email address, or contact details.
  • Sound recordings you make stay on your device. They are never uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • The free version shows ads (Google AdMob) and uses Google analytics and crash reporting. These Google services collect certain device data, described in full below.
  • A one-time in-app purchase removes all ads. Payment is processed entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment details.

1. Data collected by the app itself

None is transmitted to us. Your soundboards, tiles, recordings, imported audio, images, and settings are stored locally on your device. Board bundles you export go only where you choose to save or share them. The microphone (RECORD_AUDIO permission) is used solely for recording sounds onto tiles at your request; recordings never leave your device unless you export and share them yourself.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows banner and occasional full-screen ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • Device and account identifiers — the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • Approximate location — derived from your IP address;
  • App interactions — such as app launches and ad views/taps;
  • Diagnostics — performance and error data.

This data is encrypted in transit. Google acts as an independent ad network; its use of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices: In the European Economic Area, the UK, and other regulated regions, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change that choice anytime via Settings → Privacy options in the app. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Purchasing the one-time "Remove ads" unlock stops all ad serving and the associated ad-data collection.

3. Analytics and crash reporting (Google Firebase)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics to understand aggregate usage and fix crashes. These services collect:

  • App interactions — for example which features are used and session counts;
  • Device identifiers — including the advertising ID and app-instance identifiers;
  • Approximate region — derived from IP address;
  • Crash logs and diagnostics — stack traces, device model, OS version, and app state at the time of a crash.

Crash data is used only for crash reporting and quality improvement, and is not shared with third parties beyond Google acting as our service provider. All of this data is encrypted in transit and consists of pseudonymous identifiers — we cannot use it to identify you personally.

4. Purchases

The optional "Remove ads — Lifetime" unlock is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment and shares with us only the purchase state needed to unlock the feature (never your payment method, card number, or billing address). Purchases are tied to your Google account and restore automatically on reinstall.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • Local data (boards, recordings, settings): under your control; deleted when you delete it in-app or uninstall the app.
  • Ad and analytics data held by Google: retained per Google's published retention policies; identifiers are pseudonymous. Resetting your advertising ID unlinks past ad data from your device's future activity.
  • The app has no accounts, so there is no account to delete and no personal profile held by us. For any privacy request, contact support@superfunicular.com and we will assist, including directing deletion requests to Google's tools where the data is held by Google.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The target audience is ages 13 and up.

7. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA and similar laws)

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because the app itself holds no personal data about you, most of these rights apply to the pseudonymous data held by Google, which you can control via the in-app privacy options, your device's ad settings, and Google's My Ad Center. We honor all applicable rights requests sent to support@superfunicular.com. We do not sell personal information.

8. Changes to this policy

If our data practices change, we will update this page and the "Last Updated" date above before the change takes effect.
